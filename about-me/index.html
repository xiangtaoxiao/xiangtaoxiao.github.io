About Me

Translations:

Hi, I’m Xiangtao Xiao (Shawn). I spent four years as a product manager working on B2B commerce, payments and settlement products. I am now a full-time student in NTU’s Master of Computing in Applied Artificial Intelligence programme in Singapore.

I know how money moves through a marketplace and how to turn an ambiguous business problem into a product that ships. At NTU, I am extending that experience through hands-on work with AI systems. I am looking for a six-month internship working on AI products or applied AI, and I am available immediately.

What I bring#

Four years in product taught me how to work across the full chain: understand the business problem, make the trade-offs, align the people involved and stay with the work through launch. I have applied that approach to payment and settlement systems, B2B commerce and risk tools. I have also negotiated with banks and payment providers, and worked across finance, legal, supply chain and engineering when changes affected real money flows.

Now, as I build and compare AI systems myself, I am developing the technical judgement to assess where a model is reliable, how it should be evaluated and what it takes to turn a prototype into a usable workflow.

What I’m doing now#

Studying applied AI at NTU (Aug 2026 – Jul 2027) and building things on the side to learn by doing: local LLM pipelines, agent workflows, and small experiments in how different models actually behave given the same prompt. Most of what I learn ends up as notes on this site — AI agents, RAG, prompt engineering and transformers.
